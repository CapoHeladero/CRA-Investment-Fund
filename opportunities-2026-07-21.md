## Bear cases (the honest other side)

- **Healthcare is the weak sector.** Managed care is the only S&P group with earnings actually *falling* year-over-year, as weight-loss-drug usage pushes medical costs up. That's a real risk for UNH, ELV and OSCR even with insiders buying.
- **Breadth, not breakout.** Leadership is narrow and rotating; the index is riding a handful of names. If the S&P decisively loses its 200-day, the trend flips to neutral/down.
- **Oil is a two-way risk.** It cooled today, but any renewed Hormuz flare-up spikes energy costs and pressures the whole market's valuation at once.
- **AI concentration.** A single hyperscaler cutting its spending guidance could re-rate the entire AI complex regardless of any one company's results.
